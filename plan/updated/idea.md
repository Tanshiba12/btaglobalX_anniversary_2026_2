# BTA GLOBALx Anniversary Gala Night & Excellence Award 2026 - Block-Color Master Idea

## 1. New Creative Direction

The website is still one complete scroll experience, but the visual language changes completely.

It should no longer feel like a dark atmospheric 3D demo. It should feel like a premium playful editorial film made from bold typography, solid-color worlds, custom SVG objects, animated stickers, stamps, passes, plaques, magazine pages, and cutout-style event elements.

Reference feel:

- NVG8 / Navigate: colorful GSAP storytelling, strong character/object-led motion, solid colors, confident typography, playful transitions, and visual states that change as the user scrolls.
- Editorial event design: large type, custom layouts, campaign-like headlines, strong contrast, and clear information hierarchy.
- Premium gala restraint: even when playful, it must still feel polished enough for an awards gala, business delegates, sponsors, media partners, and international guests.

Main creative promise:

> One invitation. One day. Many stages of excellence.

The visitor should feel like they are opening a living event kit. Every act behaves like a designed print object brought to life: an RSVP stamp, invitation card, sticker map, speaker board, creative market grid, magazine launch, press wall, runway strip, award desk, registration pass board, and final memory wall.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Visual Shift

Use:

- Solid color panels.
- Sharp editorial type.
- Oversized labels and numbers.
- Chunky SVG-style shapes.
- Sticker-like icons.
- Thick borders.
- Halftone dots, paper folds, stamp marks, ticket notches, arrows, cards, cutout props.
- Shape wipes where one act color physically covers the previous act.
- Scroll-linked object transformations.

Avoid:

- Generic repeated cards.
- Heavy dark atmosphere as the default style.
- Realistic venue worlds as the main design driver.
- One design system repeated across every section.
- Copy that sounds like a placeholder or generic corporate landing page.
- Backgrounds that rely on soft blended color instead of designed blocks and objects.

## 5. Content Architecture

Primary information order:

1. Event identity, date, venue, registration.
2. Event map and highlights.
3. Forum speakers and panel topics.
4. Sustainable art and bazaar.
5. Magazine launch, media exposure, and partners.
6. Red carpet, culture, and fashion.
7. Gala dinner, awards, and lucky draw.
8. Itinerary and registration packages.
9. Gallery, contact, and loop.

Important rule:

- The site can be playful, but the practical information must be easy to find. Registration, itinerary, date, venue, contact, packages, speakers, partners, awards, and pricing cannot be hidden behind animation only.

## 6. Interaction Rules

- Hover/tap turns objects into detail previews.
- Click opens a themed overlay.
- The background stays visible behind overlays.
- Every act has at least one useful click target.
- Reduced motion shows every act as a readable editorial panel.
- Mobile uses shorter scroll, larger tap targets, and fewer moving objects.
